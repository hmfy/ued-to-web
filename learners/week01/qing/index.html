About ChargeWipe

Welcome to ChargeWipe. We are a professional service platform focusing on cross-border e-commerce chargeback early warning and risk control management. We are committed to providing full-link chargeback defense solutions for cross-border merchants, helping businesses intercept risks in advance, recover revenue losses, and maintain the health of their payment channels.

Detailed Introduction

Leveraging global mainstream chargeback alert networks and intelligent risk control technologies, ChargeWipe provides cross-border e-commerce practitioners with full-link services covering chargeback early warning, dispute resolution and fraud prevention. We deliver flexible decision mechanisms and efficient feedback tools, enabling merchants to proactively resolve disputes, lower chargeback rates, and optimize and protect the security and stability of cross-border payment channels.

Why ChargeWipe?

Stay ahead of disputes. Detect potential threats early, safeguard transactions, and help protect your revenue through intelligent risk management.

  • Multi-channel Alert Coverage

    Deeply integrates with the two global alert networks, Verifi and Ethoca, enabling early detection of high-risk transactions and covering major card schemes and payment scenarios.

  • Dual-mode Decision Engine

    Supports both manual decision-making and automatic decision-making, balancing the flexibility of manual intervention and the efficiency of system processing to fit the operational needs of merchants of all sizes.

  • Payment Channel Protection

    By intervening in disputes at an early stage, we effectively reduce merchants’ chargeback rates and prevent payment channel restrictions or increased fees caused by high-risk ratings.

  • Full-link Record Archiving

    Securely stores all records of warning reception, verification, disposal and feedback, supporting merchants’ business reviews and financial reconciliation, and enabling continuous optimization of risk control strategies.
